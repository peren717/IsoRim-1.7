Gene part models (work in progress, not loaded by the game)

isorim_gene_parts.zip   pig ears, pig nose, mini-horns, furry tail, furskin (coat for every build,
                        head fur); .obj files for Models/, renders and notes (colour rules).
make_genes.py           the Blender (bpy) script that builds them and the pawn renders:
                        python3 make_genes.py -- <output folder>
                        (uses ../WeaponModelsWIP/make_weapons.py and ../Models)

IsoRim does not draw gene parts yet: these show once code attaches them to its figures.
